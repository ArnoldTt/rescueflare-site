05 — Verification & FAQ

Trust needs evidence.

These disclosures apply to all contributions and to the speculative $RFLARE token.

Is RescueFlare a registered charity?

No registration or tax-exempt status has been independently confirmed. A transfer to this project wallet is not the same as a gift to a registered nonprofit.

Are 500 SOL confirmed as donations?

No. The organizer reports 500 SOL, but it has not been independently established as a current balance, incoming gifts, or money donated to rescue groups. The progress display is illustrative, not a verified receipt.

How can I verify the wallet?

Open the public Solscan account page. Compare the address character for character. Public wallet balances are distinct from donation totals.

Where exactly does a transfer go?

The transaction sends SOL to the stated project-controlled address, not directly to BOS Foundation, Wildlife SOS, or any NGO. The project does not currently have a verified charity partner or recipient agreement.

Is purchasing $RFLARE a donation?

No. Trading a meme token is a separate speculative activity. The token has not yet launched. No crypto trading return, charity contribution or creator-fee donation can be assumed.

How can I check the news?

The independent news links above lead to source reporting. The sources and named rescue organizations do not endorse, supervise or receive RescueFlare funds by default.
